How the planner scores a plan

Every plan gets a score in points, with your routine = 100. The score counts electricity, time spent below your comfort floors, and the heat and battery charge left at the end of the day. Each 1% better overall than your routine adds a point, so 135 is 35% better and 90 is 10% worse. A plan can cost a little more in electricity and still score higher, because it keeps you warm.
